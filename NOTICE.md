## Game data

Agent, W-Engine, Drive Disc and Bangboo names, ranks, attributes, specialties, factions, stats, set bonuses, release versions, Additional Ability names and conditions, and kit links (Ether Veil and Aftershock) in `src/data/` are factual game information compiled from the community [Zenless Zone Zero Wiki](https://zenless-zone-zero.fandom.com/) on Fandom (October 2026), whose text is available under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Details of upcoming Agents (such as Phoenix's Additional Ability condition and recommended teams) come only from HoYoverse's official reveals and livestreams; Severian's condition, expected build style and the Roxy team are estimates from the 3.3 livestream gameplay and are marked as such on the site until his official reveal. Nirvana's First Plume's base and advanced stats are from 3.3 test-server data and are marked "Subject to change" on the site until the numbers are confirmed on live servers. Skill summaries in `src/data/skills/` (skills, Core Passive values by level, Additional Abilities, Mindscapes and Potential) are shortened rewordings of the same Wiki's skill text, with the game's numbers kept, and are shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence. Bangboo skill summaries (`src/data/bangbooSkills.js`) are shortened the same way. Level-up, skill and Core Skill material costs and where they drop (`src/data/materials.js`) come from the same Wiki. Only released content and officially announced Agents are included, with occasional clearly-marked test-server figures for upcoming content, ahead of their live release; undocumented leaks are never used. No game files are extracted, decompiled or redistributed.

Recommended builds and best teams on the Agent pages (`src/data/builds.js`) are community picks compiled from [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/zenless-zone-zero/)' Zenless Zone Zero build and team guides (October 2026): only the names of W-Engines, Drive Disc sets, stats and Agents are used, not their write-ups. They are recommendations, not official HoYoverse data, and change with new Agents and patches.

Agent roles and stat targets on the Build tab (`src/data/statTargets.js`) come from the community [ZZZ Agent Build Guide](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX-1vTj2PaPq6Py_1B5fsOPj_Moc-tN_7mut7fICczI6lz1njyEIAInTnfB7lAraX4pYCRGNbaHGlIbFZ90/pubhtml) on Google Sheets; each entry notes the game version it was last updated for.
