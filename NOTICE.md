## Game data

Agent, W-Engine, Drive Disc and Bangboo names, ranks, attributes, specialties, factions, stats, set bonuses, release versions, Additional Ability names and conditions, and kit links (Ether Veil and Aftershock) in `src/data/` are factual game information compiled from the community [Zenless Zone Zero Wiki](https://zenless-zone-zero.fandom.com/) on Fandom (October 2026), whose text is available under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Details of upcoming Agents (such as Phoenix's Additional Ability condition and recommended teams) come only from HoYoverse's official reveals and livestreams, apart from Phoenix's Core Passive, Mindscapes and build guidance, which are short rewordings of pre-release information marked "Subject to change" on the site; Severian's condition and the Roxy team are estimates from the 3.3 livestream gameplay and are marked as such on the site until his official reveal; his Drive Discs and build notes come from his official 3.3 in-game trial, and his signature W-Engine Incarcerating Grip's stats and passive from that trial (as listed by zzz.gg), marked "Subject to change" until release. Phoenix and her W-Engine Plume of Rebirth released in 3.3. Sunbringer (Eldreda, Lindverne and Dracaene Sunbringer, an S-rank Fire Armorer of Roscaelifer, listed for 3.4) comes only from HoYoverse's official drip marketing; her portraits (one per member) are cropped from that official art, her blue Void Hunter Fire icon is redrawn from the one in her Agent Record (the Frost, Honed Edge and Auric Ink icons are HoYoverse's official in-game icons), the official Roscaelifer emblem (as posted by HoYoverse on HoYoLAB) and her members' department logos (Administrative Affairs and Central Computing, the latter recoloured to the gold of the official reveal) are taken from HoYoverse's "Five Central Departments" posts, and the "From a mother to her children" verses on her exported cards are quoted from the same reveal, and her kit, build and Core Skill drops are left blank until they're revealed. Kafka (an S-rank Electric Anomaly Agent from the Honkai: Star Rail collaboration, Unknown Realm, listed for 3.4) likewise comes only from HoYoverse's official drip marketing: her portrait is cropped from that art, her quote on exported cards is quoted from it, and her kit, build and Core Skill drops are left blank until they're revealed. Robin (an S-rank Ether Defense Agent from the same collaboration, Unknown Realm, listed for 3.4) comes the same way from her official drip marketing, and the team with Sunbringer and Norma from Sunbringer's official gameplay footage; the Unknown Realm logo is HoYoverse's official emblem. Kafka's and Robin's suggested builds and other teams are estimates from similar Agents (Anomaly Agents for Kafka, Zhao for Robin) and are marked as estimates on the site. Skill summaries in `src/data/skills/` (skills, Core Passive values by level, Additional Abilities, Mindscapes and Potential) are shortened rewordings of the same Wiki's skill text, with the game's numbers kept, and are shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence. Bangboo skill summaries (`src/data/bangbooSkills.js`) are shortened the same way. Level-up, skill and Core Skill material costs and where they drop (`src/data/materials.js`) come from the same Wiki. W-Engine, Bangboo EXP and Drive Disc levelling costs (`src/data/levelMats.js`) come from Icy Veins' Zenless Zone Zero leveling guide and W-Engine pages (October 2026). Faction descriptions in the Glossary (`src/data/glossary.js`) are short summaries written for this site from facts in Icy Veins' and Game8's faction guides (October 2026). The Ethereals tab's endgame data (`src/data/endgame.js`) is game information: Deadly Assault 3.3.1 dates, weaknesses, resistances and buffs as shown in game (reworded in English), and Shiyu Defense stage weaknesses, buffs, enemy waves, score targets and boss intel as shown in game (reworded in English; the October 16 rotation came from Icy Veins' Critical Node guide); boss art is cropped from HoYoverse's official art. Boss gimmicks are short summaries of the stage effects shown in game and of facts from Game8's and ZZZ Team Forge's boss guides (October 2026). The teams it suggests are picked by the site from those facts and the recommended teams above. The order to level each Agent's skills (`src/data/skillPriority.js`) follows the upgrade order listed on ZZZ Build's Agent pages (zzz-build.com, October 2026). Released content and officially announced Agents are included. For upcoming Agents (currently Sunbringer and Robin) the site also shows beta information shared by fan sites, such as kits, signature W-Engine stats, Sunbringer's element name (Genesis Flare) and builds, always marked "Beta, subject to change" with a pointer to the official ZZZ pages. Until their real art is shown, upcoming W-Engines without art use a placeholder: HoYoverse's B-rank Vortex Arrow art, recoloured and marked with a "?". No game files are extracted, decompiled or redistributed.

Recommended builds and best teams on the Agent pages (`src/data/builds.js`) are community picks compiled from [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/zenless-zone-zero/)' Zenless Zone Zero build and team guides (October 2026): only the names of W-Engines, Drive Disc sets, stats and Agents are used, not their write-ups. They are recommendations, not official HoYoverse data, and change with new Agents and patches.

Agent roles and stat targets on the Build tab (`src/data/statTargets.js`) come from the community [ZZZ Agent Build Guide](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX-1vTj2PaPq6Py_1B5fsOPj_Moc-tN_7mut7fICczI6lz1njyEIAInTnfB7lAraX4pYCRGNbaHGlIbFZ90/pubhtml) on Google Sheets; each entry notes the game version it was last updated for.
